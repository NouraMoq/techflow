# Content Creator by NovaMetrics — Packages & Pricing (Executive Summary)

> **Important:** All prices are **initial market-test prices** for a pilot launch and are **editable from the admin panel**. Prices **exclude 15% KSA VAT** where applicable. All financial and legal details **require company and specialist approval** before finalization.

## 1. Overview

**Content Creator** is NovaMetrics' Arabic-first platform that unifies everything a creator or public figure needs to run their creative and commercial journey: strategy, idea bank, content calendar, script editor, shoot-session management, reviews & approvals, commercial campaign management, contracts, and analytics — with an optional **managed service** delivered by the NovaMetrics team.

Two complementary models:
- **Self-serve software** — the client operates the tool (Launch, Pro).
- **Managed service** — NovaMetrics delivers on the client's behalf (Star, Full Management, Agency).

## 8. Not Included

External production costs (shooting/editing) unless quoted; paid ad budgets (paid directly to platforms); special equipment/locations/talent fees; specialized legal/accounting work; custom integrations beyond standard.

## 9. Upgrade / Downgrade (Proration)

- **Upgrade:** immediate, prorated for the remainder of the current cycle; higher limits activate at once.
- **Downgrade:** takes effect at the **next cycle**; no refund for the current cycle.
- Managed-tier upgrades may require a needs re-assessment and differential setup fee.

## 10. Cancellation Policy

- Monthly: cancel before renewal; service runs through the paid cycle.
- Annual: generally non-refundable after activation; auto-renewal can be disabled (**editable, needs legal approval**).
- Managed tiers: subject to an agreed notice period (e.g., 30 days) due to human-team commitments.

## 11. Content Ownership

Final creative deliverables belong to the client after payment. NovaMetrics may use selected outputs as portfolio unless otherwise agreed. Platform tools, code, and models remain NovaMetrics IP. On cancellation, clients may export data within a defined window before archival/deletion.

## 12. Custom Pricing Model

For Full Management, Agency, and out-of-bounds requests: (1) needs assessment, (2) define required human component, (3) build pricing = monthly base + setup + add-ons at ≥ 40% managed margin, (4) formal quote with deliverables and exclusions, (5) company/specialist approval.
